HOW BINDING WORKS

Face a wild creature and press Bind. The exact chance is always shown — what you see is what is rolled.

Raise your odds:
• Sneak up unnoticed (×1.5) or while it sleeps (×2)
• Weaken it first — lower health, higher chance
• Exhausted (0 HP) creatures are a guaranteed bind
• Every miss adds Resonance (+12% next time)

Bind Orbs are free and unlimited.
